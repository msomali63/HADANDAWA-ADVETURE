This 7-day adventure takes you through Tanzania's most iconic wildlife destinations. From the elephant herds of Tarangire to the endless plains of the Serengeti and the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater, this safari offers an unforgettable journey through Africa's greatest wilderness.

Travel in comfort with your own private 4x4 safari vehicle and expert guide, staying in carefully selected mid-range lodges and tented camps that blend comfort with authentic wilderness experiences.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

Follow your journey through Tanzania's greatest wildlife destinations

Day 1

Arrival in Arusha & City Tour

Welcome to Tanzania!

Upon arrival at Kilimanjaro International Airport, you will be warmly welcomed by your professional safari guide. After a short briefing, transfer to your hotel in Arusha.

In the afternoon, enjoy a guided city tour visiting the local market, a coffee plantation, and the Cultural Heritage Centre. Learn about Tanzania's rich history and culture before your safari adventure begins.

Accommodation Green Side Hotel Mid-Range Hotel in Arusha
Meals Dinner
Day 2

Arusha to Tarangire National Park

Land of Giants

After an early breakfast, depart for Tarangire National Park, renowned for its ancient baobab trees and large elephant herds. Enjoy a full day of game drives through open savannahs, riverine forests, and seasonal swamps along the Tarangire River.

Expect sightings of elephants, giraffes, buffaloes, zebras, wildebeest, lions, leopards, and a rich diversity of birdlife. A picnic lunch will be served inside the park.

Accommodation Ngare Lodge Mid-Range Lodge near Tarangire
Meals Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 3

Tarangire to Serengeti National Park

Into the Endless Plains

After breakfast, depart for the legendary Serengeti National Park. Drive through the Ngorongoro Conservation Area with stunning views of the highlands. Arrive in the Serengeti by midday and begin your first game drive in this world-famous park.

The Serengeti, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is home to over 2 million herbivores, 4,000 lions, 1,000 leopards, and 550 cheetahs. Enjoy a picnic lunch before continuing your game drive.

Accommodation Serengeti Heritage Tented Camp Mid-Range Tented Camp inside Serengeti
Meals Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 4

Full Day Serengeti Game Drive

The Great Migration

Spend a full day exploring the Serengeti's vast plains with morning and afternoon game drives. Your guide will track animal movements to maximize sightings, from big cats on the hunt to large herds of herbivores scattered across the savannah.

Depending on the season, you may witness the Great Migration, where millions of wildebeest and zebras move across the plains in search of fresh grazing. Enjoy a bush picnic lunch surrounded by the sights and sounds of the wild.

Accommodation Serengeti Heritage Tented Camp Mid-Range Tented Camp inside Serengeti
Meals Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 5

Serengeti to Ngorongoro Crater

The Eighth Wonder of the World

After breakfast, enjoy a morning game drive in the Serengeti before departing for the Ngorongoro Conservation Area. Descend into the Ngorongoro Crater, the world's largest intact volcanic caldera, for an afternoon of spectacular game viewing.

The crater floor hosts an extraordinary density of wildlife, including lions, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, hippos, and the rare black rhino. Enjoy a picnic lunch by the crater lake before ascending to your lodge on the crater rim.

Accommodation Ngorongoro Wildlife Lodge Mid-Range Lodge on Crater Rim
Meals Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 6

Ngorongoro to Lake Manyara National Park

Tree-Climbing Lions

After breakfast, drive to Lake Manyara National Park for a scenic game drive. This beautiful park is known for its lush groundwater forest, large troops of baboons, flamingos along the lake shore, and the famous tree-climbing lions.

Enjoy a picnic lunch in the park before continuing to your lodge for a relaxing evening. Lake Manyara's alkaline waters attract thousands of flamingos and an incredible variety of waterbirds.

Accommodation Manyara Best View Lodge Mid-Range Lodge near Lake Manyara
Meals Breakfast, Lunch & Dinner
Day 7

Lake Manyara to Arusha - Departure

Farewell Tanzania

After a leisurely breakfast, enjoy a final morning game drive in Lake Manyara National Park or relax at your lodge. Depart for Arusha, arriving in time for lunch at a local restaurant.

After lunch, transfer to Kilimanjaro International Airport for your departure flight. Your unforgettable 7-day Tanzania safari adventure comes to an end.

Accommodation No accommodation (End of tour)
Meals Breakfast & Lunch
